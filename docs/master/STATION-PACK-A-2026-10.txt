1. Product overview
Product name: iAgent Station Gen 1
One common platform for Home and Business; software profile changes only.
Main interaction mode: voice first, touch second, keyboard/mouse only as backup.
Target industrial approach: integrate complete modules, not redesign every subsystem.
Visual target: premium desktop terminal with 27-inch touch display and compact base.

7. Assembly concept
Mount compute board and its cooling assembly on inner frame.
Install speakers and acoustic damping parts.
Install far-field mic/DSP module in top shell acoustic zone.
Install power harness and required DC distribution.
Install RAM and NVMe according to SKU.
Close top shell on inner frame; verify cable routing and acoustic isolation.
Mount display module on bracket and hinge/arm assembly.
Connect video, touch and power through arm cable channel.
Join arm assembly to base.
Run Factory Installer, hardware QC, acoustic test and packaging.

10. Quality and validation requirements
Compute supplier: Linux boot validation, BIOS access, TPM 2.0, Secure Boot capability.
Display supplier: touch stability, HID compliance, brightness/uniformity, no ghost touch.
Voice supplier: 1 m / 3 m / 5 m capture tests, barge-in, AEC, full duplex, noise reduction.
Arm supplier: no instability under repeated touch use.
Mechanical supplier: dimensional tolerance and repeatability.
Integration supplier: final assembly checklist, serial traceability, QC report per unit.

12. Commercial note
We are open to suppliers that can provide a single module only, and also to suppliers able to manage multiple sub-assemblies. We also welcome proposals from ODMs able to integrate display + compute + mechanical, provided Linux support and our industrial design constraints are respected.
