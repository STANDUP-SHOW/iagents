Version B — Prototype propriétaire complet / industrialisation avancée
Usage du document — Ce dossier décrit l'option plus ambitieuse, destinée aux ODM, intégrateurs industriels et fabricants mécaniques capables d'accompagner une Station plus propriétaire. Il peut être envoyé seul ou en parallèle du Pack A pour comparer les approches.

1. Strategic objective
Build a recognisable iAgent Station with proprietary mechanical design, integrated acoustic architecture and a controlled supplier stack. CPU board, display module, memory, storage and DSP may remain sourced components, but the product-level integration becomes a core iAgent asset.
2. Product-level industrial target
Premium desktop AI terminal with 27-inch floating touch display
Compact iconic base in 3 finishes: Pearl White, Deep Navy, Brushed Aluminium
Voice-first interaction with integrated far-field capture and speakers
Touch-first fallback through Desktop Commander UI
Same hardware family for Home and Business

10. Assembly view — advanced architecture
Internal frame acts as structural backbone.
Compute board mounted independently from acoustic chamber.
Microphone array mounted on top shell or dedicated acoustic spine.
Speakers mounted in isolated left/right chambers.
Optional carrier board centralises LED, buttons, power distribution and service header.
Display cables routed through arm with strain relief.
Service hatch under base allows RAM/SSD replacement without full disassembly.
11. Comparison note to suppliers
We are evaluating two tracks in parallel: a fast integration track and a more proprietary product track. Suppliers may answer one or both. If a supplier proposes a staged approach (fast first prototype, then more proprietary pilot version), this is highly relevant to us.
